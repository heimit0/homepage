# Preface
## Why start a homelab
In my opinion there are only two good reasons to start a homelab. 1. Learning new skills and experimenting in a safe environment. 2. Gain independence from cloud providers. I fall under the first category.
From the start my goal was to get more comfortable with Linux, Networking and hosting my own services.

## What Equipment am I using for the Lab
My equipment started with me using my pre-existing FRITZ!Box Router and a small Mini PC from Minisforum called the MS-01 equipped with 64 GB RAM. Software-wise I'm running Proxmox VE as my hypervisor with multiple mainly Linux-based VMs.

Recently I was gifted some UniFi equipment to further expand what I could do with the Homelab.
Now the FRITZ!Box has been repurposed as a modem with the UniFi Cloud Gateway Ultra functioning as the main router, the Wi-Fi is being provided by a separate access point and a small 2.5 GbE switch connects all my other devices to the network.
